# My agent: ChefPro AI
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks create and discover meals from available pantry ingredients with a specialized catalog and deep expertise in Hyderabadi and Telangana cuisine.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Dietary restrictions, spice tolerance (crucial for Telangana heat levels), pantry staples, regional masala blends on hand, and saved favorite recipes across sessions
- Tools: Look up authentic Hyderabadi and Telangana recipes by available ingredients, suggest authentic regional ingredient substitutions (e.g., tamarind, gongura, potli masala), and save/retrieve favorite recipes
- Catalog/UI: Recipe cards (prep/dum time, spice meter, traditional cooking techniques, required vs. on-hand ingredients, step-by-step instructions) and favorites comparison tables rendered in A2UI
- Image gen: Authentic plating previews and presentation visuals for traditional dishes (e.g., dum biryanis, bagara rice, pacchi pulusu, sarva pindi, mirchi ka salan)
- Sandbox: n/a

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Traditional spice blend ratios, festive/seasonal menu planner, Cloud Trace observability
